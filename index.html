About Me

I am PhD candidate at IMPA under the guidance of Alejandro Kocsard with broad interests in Dynamical Systems, Ergodic theory and Low-dimensional Topology & Geometry. Previously, I obtained my Master's degree at the Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ), where I worked with Katrin Gelfert , and my Bachelor's degree at the Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG).

My name is pronounced exactly like "Eric". My full name is Heric Corrêa da Silva, incorporating the surnames of both my father (Corrêa) and my mother (da Silva). Professionally, I go by Heric Corrêa.
